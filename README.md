# HR-Analytics-Dashboard-PowerBI
Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard for employee attrition analysis, workforce insights, KPI reporting, and data-driven HR decision-making.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes workforce data to identify employee attrition patterns, workforce distribution trends, salary-based turnover behavior, and department-level performance indicators.

The dashboard was developed using Power BI to support HR teams and management with data-driven decision-making and workforce optimization strategies.

## Business Problem

Employee attrition impacts productivity, recruitment costs, workforce stability, and organizational growth.

The objective of this project was to identify:

- High attrition departments
- Salary-related turnover patterns
- Workforce demographic trends
- Experience-based attrition behavior
- Job satisfaction impact on retention

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel
- Data Visualization
- KPI Reporting

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Experience

## Key Insights

### Workforce Attrition
- Attrition rate reached 16.3%, indicating moderate employee turnover.

### Department Analysis
- Sales department recorded the highest attrition.

### Salary Analysis
- Employees in lower salary slabs experienced higher turnover.

### Workforce Demographics
- Majority of employees belong to the 26–35 age group.

### Experience Analysis
- Lower-experience employees demonstrated higher attrition frequency.

## Recommendations

- Strengthen retention programs in high-attrition departments.
- Review compensation strategies for lower salary bands.
- Improve employee engagement and career development initiatives.
- Introduce mentorship programs for early-career employees.

## Conclusion

The dashboard transforms raw workforce data into actionable business insights and demonstrates how HR analytics can support workforce planning, employee retention, and strategic decision-making.
